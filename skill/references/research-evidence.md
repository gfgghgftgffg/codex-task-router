# Research evidence

The search child finds sources and reports conclusions, coverage, source locations, and uncertainties. Its summary guides the parent's reading; it does not replace the underlying evidence.

Before adopting an important conclusion or choosing a direction based on research, the coordinating parent reads the complete relevant original material. Follow links or existing local files directly, including qualifications, exceptions, conflicting evidence, and surrounding context. Read the whole document when its meaning depends on the whole; do not turn this into a mandatory scan of unrelated files or every search hit. Material already read and still current need not be reread.

This judgment belongs to the main agent (Astra by default), not a substitute reviewer child. Decide whether the evidence supports the conclusion and covers the question. Ask the existing search child for specific missing evidence; absence from its search results is not proof of absence.

Read long sources in batches and track relevant sections still unread. If content is truncated, inaccessible, or available only as a snippet, state that limitation and obtain the missing material when needed. Do not represent a summary, search snippet, or partial read as a complete source review. The search role remains read-only: it returns source locations or requested original text in bounded follow-ups, rather than requiring a new artifact-writing permission.

Once the parent has enough evidence for the dependent task, pass the decision and source references to its executor, which reads the needed originals directly. Continue independent work while research is pending. This is the parent's evidence judgment, not a routine user-approval checkpoint. Known local edits and tasks with sufficient supplied evidence need no new search.
